# ChatGPT Product Lead Stream — 2026-09-08

## Executive take
Do **not** start with a classic Match-3 clone. Match-3 is proven but mature/saturated. The stronger small-team opportunity in 2026 is a **hybrid-casual puzzle** with a 1-tap/1-drag mechanic, 30–90 second levels, strong visual satisfaction, and light meta-progression.

Current signals:
- WeChat mini-game August 2026 grossing top 10 includes `向僵尸开炮` (#1) and `梦幻消除战` (#10), proving that both simple combat loops and elimination/puzzle loops monetize at scale in mini-game ecosystems.
- AppMagic 2026 market work says Match-3 revenue is essentially flat around ~$4.8B while growth is shifting to emerging puzzle formats; Sort Puzzle grew strongly, Block Puzzle monetization accelerated dramatically, and Hybrid Puzzle is the breakout direction.
- H1 2026 Hybridcasual Puzzle leaders are concentrated in **Sort, Screw, and Block** formats. Pixel Flow, Screwdom, and Color Block Jam are among the strongest benchmarks.
- Türkiye Google Play Puzzle ranking on 2026-09-07 has Color Block: Combo Blast #1, Block Blast #2, Royal Kingdom #3, Bus Traffic Fever #5. Türkiye iOS Puzzle ranking has Block Blast #1, a physics puzzle #2, Screw Match #3, Bus Traffic Fever #4.
- Important correction to prior analysis: the ~96.9% puzzle figure refers to **worldwide revenue generated by Turkish developers**, not Turkish players' domestic genre spend. Türkiye's domestic market was $347M in 2025 and puzzle was one of the fastest-growing domestic genres (+26.1% YoY).

## Top 3
### 1. Sort Flow / Buffer Queue
Why: strongest combination of 2026 market proof, simple controls, readable ads, low backend need and manageable content production. Pixel Flow-style one-tap flow + limited waiting slots is especially suitable for a tiny team.

### 2. Color Block Escape / Jam
Why: best-proven block-jam monetization format and easy to understand instantly. Main issue is saturation, so a meaningful rule twist is mandatory.

### 3. Queue / Traffic Color Dispatch
Why: Türkiye charts currently validate traffic/bus variants. Excellent fail-state creatives. Slightly more content/animation complexity than pure 2D sort flow.

## Recommended concept — working title: PORTAL FLOW
Original IP; no Harry Potter/Hogwarts references.

### Core fantasy
A chaotic arcane workshop is connected by colored portals. Small magical objects/creatures/crystals arrive in lanes. The player taps or releases them in the correct order so each reaches a same-color portal. A limited waiting tray creates the main tension.

### Core loop
1. Read 3–6 colored queues/layers.
2. Tap an exposed piece to send it into the flow.
3. If its portal is currently available, it clears with a satisfying effect.
4. Otherwise it occupies one of 4–5 temporary buffer slots.
5. Matching pieces clear buffer pressure and unlock buried pieces.
6. Clear the board to win; fill all buffer slots with no valid clear to lose.

### Why this over straight Match-3
- Understandable in <5 sec.
- One-handed.
- 2D/2.5D implementation is sufficient.
- Strong fail-state ad creatives ("one wrong tap fills the last slot").
- Levels can be data-driven and partially generated.
- Easy original theming.
- Rewarded-ad hooks feel natural (undo, +1 slot, reveal next match).
- Can ship without account/backend in v1.

## MVP spec
### Must-have
- 50 handcrafted/data-driven levels
- 5–6 colors
- 4–5 buffer slots
- 3 blockers: lock, ice/fog, chained stack
- Win/fail state
- level map or compact linear progression
- coin economy
- 3 boosters: Undo, +1 Slot, Reveal
- juice: particles, haptics, pitch-rising combo SFX, short victory sting
- local save
- privacy-friendly analytics hooks (optional until SDK choice)

### Explicitly not in MVP
- login
- guilds/social
- PvP
- backend economy
- story chapters with heavy art
- gacha
- elaborate 3D world

## First 20-level progression
1–3: 3 colors, 4 slots, pure onboarding
4–5: buried pieces / 2-layer stacks
6: first deliberate trap
7–8: 4th color
9: first lock blocker
10: booster tutorial (Undo)
11–12: mixed stack depth
13: fog hides one piece until top clears
14: 5th color
15: +1 Slot booster tutorial
16: chained pair must clear in order
17: two simultaneous portal queues
18: false-safe move / recovery opportunity
19: dense board with one Reveal opportunity
20: first “hard” level + celebratory chest

## Monetization architecture
### v1 soft launch
- Rewarded ad: +1 slot after fail
- Rewarded ad: undo 3 moves
- Rewarded ad: double level reward
- IAP: Remove Ads
- IAP: small booster pack
- No forced interstitials in first sessions

### Later
- starter offer
- streak chest
- 7-day event
- cosmetic portal skins

## Kill criteria before full production
Kill/pivot if, after a polished 20–30 level test:
- players cannot explain the rule after one tutorial level
- median first-session length < 5 minutes
- >35% of testers quit before level 5
- testers describe it as “just another sort game” and cannot name the twist
- creatives are not legible without captions
- level authoring takes >45 minutes/level on average

## Technical direction
For a real mobile store game, prefer **Unity** over Flutter for this project. Flutter + Flame can build it, but Unity has a much stronger game-specific ecosystem for animation, particles, haptics, ad mediation, IAP, profiling, device QA and future 2.5D/3D expansion. Keep the game architecture data-driven so a web prototype can still be produced quickly.
